How it works

A straightforward process from sign-in to published results.

01

Sign in

Use your registered Student ID and password. New accounts need administrator verification.

02

Review candidates

Read candidate details and manifestos for positions in an available election.

03

Cast your vote

Submit one ballot for each election you are eligible to participate in.

04

View results

Check election results after an administrator has published them.
